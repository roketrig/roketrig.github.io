Privacy Policy — 2048 Olympos

Last updated: October 29, 2026

2048 Olympos ("the game") is developed by Buğra Kasarcı, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the game handles.

Data I collect

I do not collect, store or sell any personal data myself. Your high score and settings are saved only on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device and app information, and interactions with ads.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the PRIVACY button on the help (?) screen. On iOS, you can also control tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

You can learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices and data deletion

All game data stored on your device (high score, settings) is deleted when you uninstall the game. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads; on iOS, you can turn off tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Data collected by Google for advertising is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. If you have any questions or requests about your data, contact me at the address below.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
